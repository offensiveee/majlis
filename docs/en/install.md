# Installing Majlis

[العربية](../ar/install.md) · [Back to README](../../README.md) · [Ask an AI to help](ai-help.md)

Pick your TV. Each guide starts with exactly what you need for that brand.

| | **LG** (webOS) | **Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV** | **Samsung** (Tizen) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brands | LG | Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Xiaomi, Toshiba, Sharp; Chromecast, Google TV Streamer, Nvidia Shield, Fire TV sticks | Samsung |
| You need | Free LG developer account, a computer with Node.js and Git | Nothing but the TV (the *Downloader* app from the store) | Samsung account, a computer with Tizen Studio and Node.js |
| Computer needed? | Once, for setup | No | Once, for setup |
| Time | ~10 minutes | ~5 minutes | ~30 minutes the first time |
| Phone app | Yes | Yes | No |
| Notifications over other apps | Yes | Yes | No |
| Home button opens Majlis | Yes (switches back from LG's screen) | Yes, when set as the default home app* | No (open it from Apps) |
| Pause or lower volume at adhan | Yes | Yes | — |
| Energy-saving timer | Yes | No | No |
| Guide | **[Install on LG](install-lg.md)** | **[Install on Android TV](install-android.md)** | **[Install on Samsung](install-samsung.md)** |

\* Most Android TVs let you choose Majlis as the home app. Google TV (Chromecast) and Fire TV keep their own home screen; the guide shows what to do there.

**Any other TV** with an HDMI port: plug in a Google TV or Fire TV stick and follow the Android guide.

Not sure where to start, or stuck on a step? Copy the prompt from [AI help](ai-help.md) into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and it will walk you through it.
