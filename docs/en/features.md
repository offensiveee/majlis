# Features

[العربية](../ar/features.md) · [Back to README](../../README.md)

Everything here can be changed from the phone app, and most of it from **Settings** on the TV.

## The home screen

![Home screen](../images/tv-home.jpg)

From top to bottom:
- **Name and info bar**: the majlis name (yours to change), the weather for your city, and the next occasions
- **Prayer times**: today's five prayers and sunrise; the next one is highlighted with a countdown
- **Clock and dates**: the day in Arabic and English, the Hijri date (Umm al-Qura) and the Gregorian date
- **Welcome**: a welcome line, a second line you set, and a short verse that changes every 20 minutes
- **Shortcuts**: Quran radio, guest Wi-Fi, calm mode, background and settings
- **Apps and inputs**: your apps (new ones appear automatically), then Live TV and HDMI inputs

### Remote shortcuts

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| Red / Green | Previous / next background |
| Yellow | Play or stop Quran radio |
| Blue | Show the guest Wi-Fi card |
| Home | Opens Majlis (instead of LG's launcher) |

## Prayer

![Adhan alert](../images/tv-adhan.jpg)

- Times are calculated on the TV for your city every day. Saudi cities use **Umm al-Qura**, including Isha two hours after Maghrib in Ramadan. Kuwait, Qatar and the UAE use their own official methods.
- At adhan time the home screen shows a full-screen card. If you're watching something else, a notification appears over it.
- **During the adhan** (optional): pause the video, or lower the volume, and restore it afterwards.
- **Adhan sound** (optional): choose from five recordings, previewed on the phone, or keep a soft chime.
- **Reminder** before the adhan: 5, 10 or 15 minutes.
- **Friday mode**: Dhuhr is shown as Jumu'ah, a reminder about Surat Al-Kahf appears in the morning, and Quran radio can start by itself.

## Quran radio

![Quran radio](../images/tv-radio.jpg)

Fourteen stations, including Saudi Quran Radio live, and recitations by Al-Muaiqly, As-Sudais, Ash-Shuraim, Ad-Dosari, Al-Afasy, Al-Qatami, Al-Minshawi, Al-Husary and others. Streams come from [mp3quran.net](https://mp3quran.net).

## Guests

### Guest Wi-Fi

![Guest Wi-Fi](../images/tv-wifi.jpg)

Enter the network once on the phone. Guests scan the code with their camera and join.

### Messages and occasions

![Message card](../images/tv-message.jpg)

- **Messages**: send "the coffee is ready" or anything else from the phone. It appears as a full-screen card until someone presses a button. Ready-made ones are one tap away.
- **Occasions**: add "family dinner, Thursday 9 pm". Before it, the line under the welcome counts down ("in 2 hours"). At the time, a welcome card with the occasion's name appears.
- **Islamic dates**: Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Arafah, Eid al-Adha and the Hijri new year are counted down automatically.

## Calm mode

![Calm mode](../images/tv-calm.jpg)

After a few minutes without use (5 to 30, or off), the screen becomes a slow slideshow of your backgrounds with the time, the Hijri date, a verse, the next three days' weather and the next prayer. Any button brings the home screen back. LG's own screensaver doesn't appear while Majlis is open.

## Energy saving

![Sleep countdown](../images/tv-sleep.jpg)

If no one has used the TV for a set time (15 minutes, 30 minutes, an hour or 4 hours), a one-minute countdown appears. Any button cancels it and returns to whatever was on; otherwise the TV turns off. Using the remote on the home screen, switching apps or inputs, and using the phone all count as activity.

## Settings on the TV

![Settings](../images/tv-settings.jpg)

Shows the phone address and PIN, and every option above. Use the arrows to move, OK or ◀ ▶ to change a value, and Back to close.
